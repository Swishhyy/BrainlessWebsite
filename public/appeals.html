Appeals

Ban, mute and other punishment appeals are handled in our separate appeals Discord. Everyone makes mistakes, including staff. If you think a punishment was unfair or you've learned from it, tell us there.

Join the appeals Discord

Before you appeal

  1. Read the rules and find the one you were punished for.
  2. Wait at least 24 hours after the punishment before appealing.
  3. Be honest. Appeals that lie or blame others are denied.
  4. Join the appeals Discord and open an appeal there.
  5. One appeal per punishment. Don't DM staff about it; you'll get a reply through the appeal.

Include in your appeal

  • Your in-game name at the time
  • Your account ID (Steam64 ID) if you know it
  • The punishment reason you were given, and roughly when
  • What happened, from your side, and any information relevant to your appeal
Open the appeals Discord

Most appeals are reviewed within a few days. Permanent bans for cheating are not appealable.
