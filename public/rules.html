Rules

Short version: play the game, don't ruin it for others, listen to staff. Joining the server means you agree to these rules. Click a section to expand it.

01 · General 5 rules
  1. Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, slurs or discrimination of any kind.
  2. No cheating or exploits. Hacks, macros, glitch abuse and third-party advantages are permanent bans. Report bugs, don't farm them.
  3. Follow staff instructions. If you disagree with a decision, take it to an appeal, not the game.
  4. No advertising. Don't promote other servers, communities or services.
  5. Appropriate names and sprays. Nothing offensive, NSFW or impersonating staff.
02 · Gameplay 6 rules
  1. No mass RDM. Killing is part of the game, but killing your own team or random players for no reason is not.
  2. No teaming between enemy factions. SCPs, Class-D/Chaos and Foundation forces play their own objectives. Short truces are fine; long-term teaming isn't.
  3. Don't camp or stall the round. Hiding forever to drag out a round you can't win isn't fun for anyone.
  4. Respect cuffed players. Don't kill compliant detainees. Detainees should follow reasonable orders.
  5. No ghosting. Don't pass information from spectator or other channels to living players.
  6. Rogue roles are the exception. Rogue Guards and Rogue Scientists are hostile to everyone by design; fighting them is not RDM.
03 · Voice & chat 4 rules
  1. No mic spam. No earrape, soundboards or music over voice chat.
  2. English in global channels. So staff and other players can follow along.
  3. SCP proximity chat is in-character. Have fun with it, but the respect rule still applies.
  4. No spamming the text chat or intercom.
Punishments are at staff discretion and scale with severity and history: warning, kick, temporary ban, permanent ban. Think a punishment was unfair? Submit an appeal.
