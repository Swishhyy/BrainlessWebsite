// Server & Discord rules

Rules

Joining our SCP:SL server or our Discord means you agree to these rules. Listen to staff, respect each other, and don't ruin it for others.

SCP:SL server rules Discord rules
You must be 16 or older. Players under 16 are not allowed on our SCP:SL server or in our Discord, and will be banned.

SCP:SL server rules

Northwood's rules and terms also apply.

Server rules 16 rules
  1. No Discrimination. No racism, homophobia, discriminatory jokes, or slurs, including partial or disguised slurs. This includes names and baiting others into saying them.
  2. No False Reports. Do not submit knowingly false or malicious reports. Honest mistakes and good-faith reports are allowed, even when staff find no rule violation.
  3. No Staff Impersonation. Do not pretend to be server staff or Northwood staff, or use names and badges intended to mislead players about your authority.
  4. No Mic Spam in Global Channels. No excessively loud audio, music spam, or disruptive mic spam in radio, SCP team chat, spectator chat, or the intercom. Global means any non-proximity voice channel. Proximity chat does not count as global chat. Follow staff volume warnings.
  5. No Deliberate SCP Suicide. Do not deliberately disconnect or die to hazards to abandon your SCP or zombie role. Accidental deaths, genuine connection problems, and crashes are not violations.
  6. English in Public Chats. Use English in public voice and text chats so players and staff can understand you.
  7. No Killing Compliant Detainees. Human players must not execute compliant cuffed prisoners. Warn prisoners and give them a chance to comply before killing for disobeying clear, reasonable instructions or attempting to escape. Immediate self-defence is allowed. Cuffing someone yourself is not permission to execute them.
  8. No Round Delaying. Do not deliberately stall a round by hiding indefinitely, refusing to progress, or excessive camping. Tactical hiding and defensive play are allowed when they help progress the round.
  9. Follow Staff Directions. Follow reasonable staff directions during moderation. If you disagree with a decision, appeal through our appeals Discord after the situation is handled. Staff instructions do not override Northwood's rules.
  10. No Harassment. No targeted bullying, sexual harassment, intimidation, or repeated personal abuse. Do not follow players between rounds or platforms to continue harassing them.
  11. No Cheating or Exploit Abuse. No cheats, unfair client advantages, item duplication, out-of-bounds abuse, or deliberately exploiting broken custom roles, items, or weapons. Report bugs privately to staff through our community Discord.
  12. No Ghosting. Do not use Discord, streams, screen sharing, or spectator information to give living players an unfair advantage. Discord calls are allowed. Communication through intended in-game mechanics is allowed.
  13. No Deliberate Team Griefing. Do not intentionally sabotage your team through grenade or Tesla kills, trapping teammates, or repeatedly interfering with them. Genuine accidents and ordinary mistakes are not griefing.
  14. No Punishment Evasion. Do not use alternate accounts, reconnects, or other methods to bypass bans, staff-issued voice mutes, or other moderation restrictions.
  15. No Threats or Sharing Private Information. No real-world threats, doxxing, or sharing someone's private information without their permission. This includes threatening attacks against players or the server.
  16. No Staff Power Abuse. Staff must not use administrative powers to win ordinary rounds or give friends an unfair advantage. Special event changes must be clearly announced. Report suspected staff abuse through our community Discord.

Discord rules

Discord 11 rules
  1. Follow Discord's Terms of Service.
  2. Listen to staff at all times.
  3. Be respectful at all times.
  4. Keep everything in its respective channel.
  5. No slurs, hate speech, racism, NSFW or gore. These are strictly prohibited at all times, in any form.
  6. No drama. Don't bring drama from other servers or your personal life into ours.
  7. Don't @ or ping staff for no reason.
  8. Don't message staff outside of the Discord unless they've added you.
  9. Don't ask for special roles or treatment of any kind.
  10. No spamming in any channel. Just have common sense and don't do it.
  11. Report with evidence. If someone breaks the rules, try to provide evidence of some kind. It makes our job easier and quicker.
Punishments are at staff discretion and scale with severity and history: warning, kick, temporary ban, permanent ban. Think a punishment was unfair? Appeal in our appeals Discord.
