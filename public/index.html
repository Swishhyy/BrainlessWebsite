// About us

What makes Brainless different

Most rounds feel familiar until they don't. Roughly 80% of spawns roll a custom role, every SCP has variants, and the facility is full of gear you won't find on vanilla servers.

🎭

Custom roles

Locksmith Class-D who pick keycard doors, Mad Scientists with revolvers, MTF Swordsmen with SCP-1509, Chaos Juggernauts, and rare all-human rolls like Resilient and Big Boi.

🧟

SCP variants

Explosive zombies, a Doctor that revives the dead, venomous 939s, a 173 that explodes on death, a 096 that's always angry. Every round the SCPs play a little differently.

🧪

Items & weapons

Cluster and impact grenades, sticky containment foam, hacking chips, reinforcement radios, five flavours of SCP-500, a grenade launcher and a stun gun.

⚙️

SCP-914 crafting

Feed grenades, pills, keycards and radios into 914 to craft custom gear. Step in on Rough if you feel lucky.

🎙️

SCP proximity chat

Some SCPs can talk to nearby players. Negotiate, threaten, or just say hi before the jumpscare.

📈

XP & stats

Earn XP for kills, escapes and crafting, track your playtime, and climb the leaderboard. Link Discord for /pb-stats.

// Round events

When things get weird

Rare special rounds that need a full-ish server, and never two in a row.

🦠

Infection

Low-health SCP-3114s hunt the humans. Anyone they kill gets back up as one of them.

⚔️

Deathmatch

Chaos has taken the facility and MTF is raiding it back. Waves every 15 seconds; first to 40 kills wins.

🦾

Juggernaut

One heavily armored Chaos Juggernaut against every NTF hunter. Kill it and you become the next one.
